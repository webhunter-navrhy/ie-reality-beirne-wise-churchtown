No. 41 Trimbleston is a most attractive, second floor apartment located in front of the development in the Sycamore block. Trimbleston is a prestigious, Sorahan-built development off the Goatstown Road, considered a most desirable location in south Dublin. This modern development comprises a lovely blend of apartments and houses set amidst landscaped gardens. This owner-occupied unit has been well maintained throughout and features accommodation extending to 78sq.m (excluding the balcony); it comprises an entrance hall with a storage closet and hot press, a kitchen that is open plan with the living/dining room, two bedrooms (one en-suite), and a family bathroom. This superb apartment has a generous balcony overlooking private landscaped gardens and is accessible from the living/dining room and master bedroom.

Trimbleston enjoys a central location; it is within close proximity to Clonskeagh, Milltown, Ranelagh, Dundrum, and Stillorgan, offering an excellent range of shopping centres, speciality shops, restaurants, eateries, schools and sporting amenities. It is within walking distance of UCD. Access to the M50/N11 motorways are close by and the No. 11 bus goes to the City Centre.

There are communal gardens with green areas, planted beds and cobble lock pathways throughout the development, and there is a fountain in the main garden. There are paved off street parking spaces for visitors and underground parking
